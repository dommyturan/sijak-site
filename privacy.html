PRIVACY

Sijak privacy policy · last updated November 16, 2026

Where your information is kept

Sijak runs on your own computer or Raspberry Pi. Players, match records and settings are saved on that device. They are not sent to the makers of Sijak or to anyone else.

Google Drive backups

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Sijak asks Google for one permission: access to only the files Sijak creates (the Google Drive "drive.file" permission). With it, Sijak:

  • creates a folder called "Sijak backups" in your Google Drive,
  • saves backup files into that folder, and deletes its own older backups when you set a limit,
  • lists and downloads those backup files when you choose to restore one,
  • reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which account is connected.

Sijak cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive. Your backups go straight from your device to your own Google Drive; they never pass through a server run by the makers of Sijak.

The sign-in from Google is stored only on your Sijak device. You can disconnect at any time in Sijak (Settings, Backups, Disconnect), which also cancels the permission with Google, or remove Sijak from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Use of Google user data

Sijak's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to make and restore your backups. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
